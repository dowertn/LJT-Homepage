Hello! I’m Junteng Liu, a first-year PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in 2024. I have worked as a research intern at MINIMAX, Tencent WXG, and Shanghai AI Lab.

Publications (recorded here in brief; full entries are on the Publications page) include the first-author works "SynLogic: Synthesizing Verifiable Reasoning Data at Scale for Learning Logical Reasoning and Beyond" and "On the Perception Bottleneck of VLMs for Chart Understanding", as well as the first-author paper "On the Universal Truthfulness Hyperplane Inside LLMs" (EMNLP 2024). I also co-authored "In-Context Sharpness as Alerts: An Inner Representation Perspective for Hallucination Mitigation" (ICML 2024), "C-Eval: A Multi-Level Multi-Discipline Chinese Evaluation Suite for Foundation Models" (NeurIPS 2023), and "Composing Parameter-Efficient Modules with Arithmetic Operations" (NeurIPS 2023).
